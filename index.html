Passively Morphing Aerial-Ground Robot

A palm-sized robot that flies over obstacles and rolls through tight spaces, switching between flight and driving using only its own thrust.

Problem

Robots that explore tight, unpredictable spaces face a hard tradeoff. Flying clears obstacles but drains the battery, while driving is efficient but gets stopped by a single step. Doing both usually means adding a separate motor to switch modes, which is heavy, power-hungry, and one more part to fail.

Exploded view of drone arms and tracks with folding demonstration

Solution

A palm-sized robot that flies to clear obstacles and drives to cover ground, switching between the two on its own.

1

The bistable hinge

No motor does the transforming: propeller thrust snaps it up into flight, and the jolt of landing folds it back down to drive.

2

Two ways to move

It flies like a quadrotor to jump obstacles, then rolls on tank tracks to cover ground without draining the battery.

3

A fraction of the size

At just 200 grams it fits in your palm, making it 3 to 30 times lighter than other robots that both fly and drive.
